The solution

A card for the agent. Keys, not card numbers.

You issue it under your debit card. The private key never leaves the agent. The bank holds the public key, bound to limits you approve.

Step 1

Pick an agent. Approve once with Face ID. Set the limits.

It starts in the bank app. Choose Grok Bot, Muse, Dots or Hermes, confirm with Face ID, then set a max amount, max payments or unlimited, a validity window — including one year or no expiry — and whether only trusted merchants are allowed. If a basket doesn’t match, we pause and ask you. The agent can buy different things each time; there is no fixed “purpose” on the card.

Step 2

The agent signs every payment with its own key.

A one-time deep link opens the agent on this phone. It creates the key pair; the private key never leaves the agent. Harbor Bank stores only the public key. Every payment is a signed response to a bank challenge — nonce, amount, merchant, and your original request.

Step 3

Unknown merchant? Paused, for your OK.

Unverified shops are never auto-declined. You see a risk score and “Did you mean ElectroHub (electrohub.ae)?” You decide. Repeated declines feed a shared blocklist.

Step 4

The receipt carries your own words. One tap revokes the rest.

Delegate your purchases without fear. We’ll remind you where each one came from, in your own words — even if the statement says EHUB TRADING LLC DXB. Revoke the Agent Card in one tap; your Visa Debit •• 4410 is unchanged. The audit log is the dispute evidence.

Native integration

Native to the bank app. Works with any agent.

Issued under your existing card, with the same Face ID you already use. Connect Grok Bot, Muse, Dots, Hermes — or any agent that can hold a key.
